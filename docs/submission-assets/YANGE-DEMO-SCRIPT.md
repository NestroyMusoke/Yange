## Complete voiceover

I haven't always had people around who noticed the small things. And I know what it feels like to go without that kind of attention.

So every morning, standing in front of clothes I had forgotten I owned, I started wondering: what would it feel like to be met by something that actually paid attention? Not to fix me. Not to judge me. Just to notice.

That question became Yange. I'm Musoke Nestroy, and I built it alone in Kampala. In Luganda, *yange* means mine: my wardrobe, my evidence, my style.

Yange starts simply: I take a photograph of my trousers.

Gemini examines what is visible and proposes the garment type, colour and material cues. At the same time, a lightweight model removes the background in my browser, giving every item a clean, consistent place in my wardrobe even when the original photographs look different.

Those jobs are deliberately separate. The cutout improves presentation, but it never becomes evidence and cannot change what Yange believes about the garment.

Next, I photograph the care label. Symbols that people often ignore or struggle to understand become usable information. Gemini extracts the fabric composition, washing limits, bleaching rules and drying instructions into a structured record I can inspect.

Yange never silently turns an AI guess into truth. It shows me what it found, exposes uncertainty and waits for confirmation. AI proposes; I confirm; deterministic rules commit.

Once saved, these are no longer two disconnected photographs. They become one evidence-backed garment in my wardrobe memory: what it is, what it looks like and how it should be cared for.

When I mark the trousers worn, Yange does more than change a badge. It updates their availability and carries the same confirmed care evidence into their next state.

This recording ends there, but the workflow continues. In Laundry, Yange uses those saved instructions to block unsafe pairings, build compatible wash groups and preserve the correct drying method. Uncertain garments are held for review instead of being placed into a risky load.

That is the larger idea: capture something once, verify it once, then let that memory keep working. One photograph becomes a clean digital garment. One care label becomes trusted evidence. And one simple “worn” action turns that evidence into help exactly when it matters.

Because I wanted Yange to feel personal—like a friend who takes the time to learn you—I made that learning visible. Style Aura is the cherry on top: with every confirmed wear and confidence check-in, its colours move gradually toward what makes you feel most like yourself. Not instantly. Gradually. Until Yange becomes unmistakably yours.

This is not a chatbot attached to a closet. Gemini handles ambiguity. Deterministic policy protects truth. Google Cloud keeps it running. But none of that was the point. The point was building the kind of attention I know what it's like to go without.

Yange. Your wardrobe, paying attention. Built alone in Kampala by Musoke Nestroy.

## How to narrate the 2:04 product recording

The product narration is **289 words**: approximately 2 minutes 4 seconds at 140 words per minute. Read it conversationally, but keep the pauses short until the final three sentences.

- **Opening:** begin “Yange starts simply” as the real trousers image first appears.
- **Background removal:** land “clean, consistent home” on the clearest before-and-after frame.
- **Care label:** begin “Next, I photograph the care label” as the second photograph appears.
- **Extracted result:** say “AI proposes; I confirm” while the structured fields or confirmation control are visible.
- **Worn state:** begin “When I mark the trousers worn” on the exact tap, not before it.
- **Laundry bridge:** begin “The demo ends here” after the visible state change. This wording honestly signals that Laundry is the next workflow rather than something currently on screen.
- **Final line:** land “when it matters” on the last frame before the Style Aura cut.

Do not add Laundry screenshots over the 1:02 recording. The spoken bridge, README, live link and full product submission can carry that proof without confusing the visual chronology.

## Style Aura delivery

- Say **“like a friend who takes the time to learn you”** warmly, without sounding apologetic.
- Pause after **“Not instantly.”**
- Say **“Gradually.”** over the clearest visible colour movement.
- Land **“unmistakably yours”** exactly as the Aura reaches its strongest frame.
- Do not explain WebGL, interpolation or the eight-percent guardrail here. The architecture material and technical proof can carry those details; this moment should communicate why the feature exists.

## Sound and proof rules

- Keep the opening nearly silent, then introduce the music when the real Yange interface appears at 0:43.
- Use subtitles throughout: one or two lines, sentence case, high contrast, no karaoke animation.
- Label the generated opening footage discreetly as **Dramatized visual**.
- Music should be almost inaudible during the 23-second Google Cloud proof clip.
- Do not overlay decorative captions on controls or logs that judges need to inspect.
- Leave the live URL on screen for the final eight seconds.
